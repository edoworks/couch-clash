**Name:** Couch Clash

**Subtitle:** Five calls. One couch.

**Description draft:**

Couch Clash adds a little friendly rivalry to football night. Pass one phone around your group, make three quick calls before kickoff, and come back for two more at halftime. Then put the phone down and enjoy the game.

Your host names the teams and confirms what happened on the broadcast. Pick a winner, call the first score, and boost one pregame prediction. Equal scores share the win. The final recap brings everyone’s calls together.

• Two to six people on one device
• Five football predictions, with clear locks and scoring
• A fictional demo to learn by playing
• Local saved games and recaps
• No account, money, prizes or betting

Custom games are host-defined: schedules and outcomes are not independently verified. There is no live score feed or phone-to-phone synchronization. Game records stay in this app installation; website and app saves are separate. The host must close unsubmitted cards as soon as play begins. This is friendly pass-and-play, not a server-enforced competition.

**Review notes draft for a future evergreen build:**

No sign-in is required. Choose “Host another football game.” Enter two distinct team labels and two or more player names. For an actual upcoming broadcast, confirm that the game has not started, then start the manual match. Pass the device to each named player; select or explicitly skip all three calls and lock each card. The host’s “play started” control closes unfinished cards as skips. At halftime, enter and review the first two outcomes before confirming them. Players then make two halftime calls. At full time, confirm the final three outcomes to show the group recap. Save the final game to revisit it locally.

For testing without a broadcast, use “Try a quick demo” and advance its clearly fictional outcomes. It is not a live match. The older scheduled fixture retains its original kickoff restrictions; creating a custom match does not reopen those saved picks.
